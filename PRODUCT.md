# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in the UAE who are vegan, plant-based, vegan-curious, or looking for kinder places, events, recipes, guides, and local community.

## Product Purpose

Dubai Vegan Community helps people discover plant-based life across the UAE and gives local businesses, event organizers, and community members a place to contribute.

## Positioning

It combines a useful local guide with the warmth and immediacy of an active community rather than behaving like a static directory.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current scope is one complete desktop homepage designed in Paper.
- Existing Paper frames `hero - blurry` and `Blurry — Community Week` are approved visual anchors and must remain untouched.
- The homepage should clearly route visitors toward places to eat, events, guides, stories, and contribution.
- Copy must not invent commercial claims, testimonials, or unverified metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Dubai Vegan Community name and existing logo assets.
- Use Urbanist with system sans-serif fallback. Mansalva is optional and limited to a genuinely useful human accent.
- No Sora, decorative italics, eyebrow text, numbered section chrome, purple or indigo gradients, or em dashes.
- Extend the painterly, blurry, botanical visual language already approved in Paper.

## Evidence on Hand

The Paper file `Kindhearted nest` contains the approved hero, approved community-week section, navigation, logos, and an image reference collection with painterly greenery, flowers, ducklings, a sheep, and community imagery. No verified testimonials, venue claims, or performance metrics were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make local discovery immediately understandable.
- Balance emotional community imagery with genuinely useful navigation.
- Keep the two blurry anchor sections special by placing quieter editorial sections between them.
- Welcome curious newcomers without preaching or moral pressure.
- Invite contribution so the community feels alive rather than published at people.
